# Bambika v1.0 Full Mockups (text-based)

All screens use design tokens: Primary Purple (#6B21A8), Accent Yellow (#EAB308), White backgrounds, Purple‑light (#F3E8FF) cards.

## GameScreen
- Purple gradient background with a large rounded card in the centre.
- Card shows a question mark until "Tap to Draw" is pressed; then displays the question text.
- Below the card: two main buttons – "Truth" (purple, left) and "Drink/Dare" (yellow, right; forced to "Dare" in Sober Mode).
- Small conditional buttons: "Skip" (grey, left of centre) and "Undo" (grey, top right).
- Timer bar at the bottom: thin yellow line that animates from full width to zero.

## ResultsScreen
- White background; large trophy icon at the top.
- Heading "Game Over!" in purple.
- Player ranking list: coloured avatar circle, player name, drink count, and a ranking label (e.g. "Most Drinks", "Most Honest").
- Session stats: cards played, deck used, duration.
- Three action buttons: "Share Results" (yellow, full width), "Replay" (purple outlined), "New Game" (text only).

## DeckEditor
- White background with a header containing:
  - Deck name text input and an emoji picker button.
  - Category pills (horizontal scroll) and a "+ Add Category" button.
- Card list (scrollable): each row shows a question preview and an intensity badge; swipe‑to‑delete reveals a red delete button.
- Floating action button ("+") at the bottom‑right to add a new card.
- Footer toolbar: "Share QR" (purple, disabled when free) and "Delete Deck" (red text).
